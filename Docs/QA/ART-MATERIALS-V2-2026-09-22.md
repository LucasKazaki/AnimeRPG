## Material deliverable

The v2 generator creates four original 512 x 512 tileable material families: limestone, concrete, brushed steel and asphalt. Each has base color, tangent-space normal, ORM and height maps, for 16 PNGs total plus a manifest. The maps are standalone rather than atlas tiles, eliminating the first pack's atlas-gutter/repeat problem.

`Content/Starter/MaterialsV2/expected-manifest.json` pins the exact generated file sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Generated binaries stay outside Git in this slice because the engine has no importer/render path yet. No third-party texture pixels or generated-service outputs are present. These are procedural calibration/default sources, not artist-finished hero materials.

The regression suite covers a valid pack, payload corruption rejection, a rehashed semantic metallic-channel error, manifest traversal rejection, overwrite refusal and exact read-only regeneration checking. The validator checks file-set integrity, SHA-256, PNG signature/chunk/CRC/decode, 512 x 512 RGB8 format, sRGB versus linear declaration, exact horizontal/vertical seam continuity, normal Z sanity, binary material-consistent metallic values and non-promoted runtime status.

A fixed-metadata temporary TAR.XZ packaging roundtrip was inspected for safe regular members, extracted into a new owned directory, revalidated and byte-checked against generator output. That archive was test evidence only, not repository source.

## Author visual inspection

A local 4 x 4 contact sheet was inspected for base-color, normal, ORM and height outputs. Limestone and concrete show restrained large/mid-frequency variation; brushed steel shows deliberate anisotropic brushing; asphalt remains dark and rough. No directional lighting is baked into base color. ORM visualizations look yellow/magenta when shown as RGB because their channels store data rather than display color.

This inspection is not independent review. It does not establish photorealism, anime-style runtime response, correct mip/compression behavior or professional material quality.

## Research decisions

- Blender **5.2.2 LTS** is the proposed primary production DCC because the current LTS line is supported through July 2028 and its current glTF exporter documents triangle conversion and metal/rough workflows.
- glTF **2.0.1** is retained as the preferred future interchange candidate, not an implemented engine feature. Its core conventions align with this pack: base color sRGB, tangent-space +Y normals, occlusion R, roughness G and metallic B.
- UE 5.8 and Unity 6 references confirm metal/rough material inputs are relevant top-tier comparisons, but no parity is claimed.
