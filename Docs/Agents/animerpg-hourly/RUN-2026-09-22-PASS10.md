## Implemented increments

- **GAME-047, deterministic enemy attack plans:** training enemies now produce bounded `QuickCut`, `GuardBreaker`, and `RiftBurst` plans with explicit windup, damage, guard damage, blockability, and recovery. Queueing is single-flight and cannot bypass recovery.
- **GAME-048, aggression presets:** `Relaxed`, `Standard`, and `Aggressive` alter enemy recovery cadence without inflating health or mutating an attack already in flight. Invalid enum values fail closed.
- **GAME-049, boss pressure rotation:** the existing Boss profile now changes its attack sequence in Pressure phase. Normal alternates QuickCut and GuardBreaker; Pressure adds the unblockable RiftBurst to a three-step sequence.
- **GAME-050, stagger interruption:** reaching the existing stagger threshold cancels a queued enemy windup and prevents an immediate requeue until the stagger window ends.
- **GAME-051, defense-earned punish opening:** a resolved perfect defense arms one one-second punish opening. The next valid direct hit gains a bounded 5/4 damage multiplier; misses and cooldown rejection do not consume it, and expiry removes it.
- **QOL-011, selectable boss practice phase:** Boss training can lock directly to Normal or Pressure phase at full training health. Reset preserves the selected practice phase; clearing it returns normal health-driven phase behavior. Non-Boss and invalid phase selections fail closed.

These are game-domain mechanics in the existing production `CombatSandbox`, not a parallel combat framework. No party-switch/gacha/PvP scope is introduced.
