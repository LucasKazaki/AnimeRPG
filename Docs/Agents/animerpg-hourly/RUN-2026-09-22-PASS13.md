## Implemented game changes

- `GAME-062`: maximum-six deterministic sequence over existing QuickCut, GuardBreaker, and RiftBurst patterns. Cursor advances only on a successful queue.

- `GAME-065`: bounded score report separates base execution points from a 125/100/75 percent session-active-time coefficient. Hits grant no positive base points but count in resolved-attempt timing. Hit-only reports still expose their timing coefficient, and direct resets of the mutable `CombatSandbox` clock cannot rewind or improve an already-earned score tier.
- `GAME-066`: non-free drill goals accept 1 through 10 required successes; Free Practice remains objective-free.
- `QOL-014`: QuickCut/GuardBreaker/RiftBurst attempts, perfect/ordinary defenses, hits, interruptions, and damage are tracked separately and reset without changing practice configuration.

The first Codex review on `ca187fb9c03d6e2ffe873599bf28da6f75b43880` found three material issues. The first repair series:

The next Codex review on exact head `126b6eee606a27605b9812fb79e554f32bfd34e6` found two additional scoring edge cases. The second repair series:

A regression first earns the 75% tier, resets the combat sandbox clock, and requires the coefficient and score to remain unchanged.
