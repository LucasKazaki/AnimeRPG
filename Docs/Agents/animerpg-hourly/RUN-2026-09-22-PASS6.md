## Verification and review repairs

Focused GCC C++17 `-Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic` passed on an earlier challenge head. Clang ASan/UBSan initially exposed signed overflow in Balanced tactical-count aggregation; widened and saturating arithmetic fixed it and the rerun passed. Because later review repairs changed production code, those earlier focused runs are not claimed as final-head evidence.

Independent Codex review found and drove seven concrete verification/design repairs:

1. The original standalone challenge regression executable was not registered with hosted CTest. Pure challenge-rule cases now run in the already-registered `LandmarkInteractionTests`, and the duplicate unregistered test was removed without touching CMake owned by engine PR #13.
2. Apex initially reused Expert lower-rank boundaries. Apex now has explicit stricter 2/3/4-goal behavior with registered boundary coverage.
3. Direct challenge-tracker tests did not prove `LandmarkEncounter` integration. The active packet now explicitly permits the already-registered `LandmarkEncounterTests`, which configures real challenges and verifies first-clear reward request/application, retry persistence, and full-resource-cap behavior through production `LandmarkEncounter::Update` and `Retry` paths.
4. The packet originally failed to list `LandmarkEncounterTests` as an allowed path even though that integration coverage was necessary. The packet scope was expanded before the final integration repair work, preserving the repository's source-of-truth ordering.
5. Challenge completion originally consumed whole-session training counters and score, allowing combat before Lincoln encounter activation to satisfy tactical/variety goals and inflate encounter rank. `LandmarkEncounter` now snapshots training stats at activation and retry, computes nonnegative damage/technique/counter deltas, and applies the existing fast/standard/slow timing coefficient to encounter-local score only. Registered integration coverage performs an Eclipse before activation, then proves that reaction cannot satisfy encounter tactical/variety goals or contribute to the encounter score.
6. A pre-activation technique could still influence the multiplier of the first different post-activation technique. For configured challenges only, activation now clears `CombatSandbox`'s transient technique-chain count/time/type before snapshotting metrics; cumulative training statistics remain intact, and challenge-disabled activation preserves the prior chain behavior.
7. Review then identified that the chain-isolation behavior itself lacked a registered regression. Commit `a4143af3292b342e891cf9ecdfaf07e0fc5b4133` extends the already-registered `LandmarkEncounterTests` to prime a pre-activation Reaction, activate a configured encounter, trigger a post-activation Stagger, verify standalone Stagger technique scoring rather than a carried multiplier, verify cumulative statistics are preserved for activation-local deltas, and separately prove an unconfigured encounter does not reset the chain.
