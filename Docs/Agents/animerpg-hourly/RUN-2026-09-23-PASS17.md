## Implemented increments

- `GAME-082`: `ShadowbladeActions` now owns the merged `ShadowbladeLoadout` and exposes it through the game-domain owner. Training-Blade-only tuning is intentionally baseline-neutral.
- `GAME-083`: net loadout Attack raises Fatal Strike damage at 2 damage per point, hard-capped at +40.
- `GAME-084`: net Mobility raises dash distance by 0.25 units per point, hard-capped at +2.0; direction normalization and fallback behavior are unchanged.
- `GAME-085`: net Resource Recovery raises Shadow regeneration by 1 resource/s per point, hard-capped at +10/s; invalid deltas remain no-ops and total resource remains capped at 100.
- `GAME-086`: net Guard reduces blockable non-perfect guard-integrity damage by 2 per point, capped at 30, and the already-existing family resonance bonuses flow into the same live tuning. Unblockable and perfect-defense semantics remain separate.
- `QOL-018`: `PreviewPresetTuning` copies the loadout, applies the saved preset to the copy, returns the authoritative `LoadoutActionResult`, reports exact effective action tuning on success, and preserves both current equipment and caller output on failure.

The first five increments are wired into the existing `ShadowbladeActions` game flow. The comparison preview is a production-path API for a future UI but has no rendered/native caller yet. Equipment selection itself still has no native UI/input binding. Therefore this receipt does not claim that advanced loadouts are player-selectable or natively playable.
