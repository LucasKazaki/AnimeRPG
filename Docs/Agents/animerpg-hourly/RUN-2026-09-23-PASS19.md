- Shadow Momentum saturates at three and is granted only by Perfect Guard/Perfect Dodge.
- One otherwise-valid Fatal Strike consumes at most one stack for +12 damage; all pre-activation rejections preserve momentum.

- CryoEdge Perfect Guard restores up to 20 Guard Integrity, capped at 100.
- Training Blade Perfect Dodge reduces current dash cooldown by up to 0.5 seconds, clamped at zero.
- `CurrentActionReadiness()` exposes finite/clamped resource/cooldown telemetry, normalized cooldowns, base affordability, guard state, and current momentum without mutation.
- Existing transient reset preserves loadout while clearing new momentum state.
