## Review-driven repairs

Independent Codex review materially strengthened this packet. Resolved findings required:
1. binding Warden entry to the same persistent protagonist that completed Shadow Crypt;
2. preventing missed stagger openings from farming higher personal-best scores;
3. saturating pathological miss/defense counters before signed score overflow;
4. preventing saturated miss evidence from falsely winning later personal-best tie-breakers;
5. explicit positive-infinity, negative and NaN timing no-mutation coverage;
6. all nine difficulty-by-phase timing windows with exact-boundary and just-outside behavior;
7. complete Story-record preservation across a Standard clear;
8. a worse replay on the same trial proving monotonic personal-best retention;
9. full practice-mode record/unlock isolation while Expert is still locked; and
10. treating capped damage as information-loss evidence so an actually worse capped-damage replay cannot replace the established record only because its stored clear time is shorter.

The final strengthened registered suite also rejects invalid difficulty/response/phase enums, verifies a stagger punish at the exact two-second boundary is consumed exactly once, and reproduces the capped-damage ordering edge by comparing a slower 9999-damage baseline with a faster replay that takes 100 additional real hits after the same stored cap.
